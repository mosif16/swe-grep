# Participation expectations

Keep SWE-Grep issues, reviews and project discussions respectful and focused on
the work. Explain technical disagreements with evidence and give others room to
correct mistakes. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or impersonate
participants, or disclose private information without permission. Avoid spam and
unrelated promotion.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation
when needed to keep project spaces useful. Report concerns privately to
[msayf@recordandlearn.info](mailto:msayf@recordandlearn.info), including relevant
links and only the details needed to explain the issue. Do not publish another
person's private report. Security vulnerabilities use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
